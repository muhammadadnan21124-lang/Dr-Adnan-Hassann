About Me
Executive Biography

Dr. Adnan, BDS, is a Bangladeshi dental surgeon, humanitarian healthcare advocate, and emerging global health leader whose professional journey integrates clinical dentistry, special care, community healthcare, public health advocacy, and international collaboration. His work is driven by a commitment to delivering patient-centered dental care while expanding access to essential healthcare for underserved populations.

Currently practicing at Khan Dental, Kalachandpur, Gulshan-2, Dhaka, Bangladesh, Dr. Adnan has developed clinical competencies across restorative, aesthetic, preventive, surgical, and rehabilitative dentistry. His clinical practice emphasizes comprehensive dental assessment, evidence-informed treatment planning, patient safety, and individualized care.

Clinical Expertise & Areas of Practice

Dr. Adnan's clinical competencies include:

  • Restorative & Aesthetic Dentistry Direct composite restorations, aesthetic restorations, and rehabilitation of damaged teeth.
  • Endodontics Root canal treatment and management of endodontic conditions.
  • Oral Surgery Simple and surgical tooth extractions and emergency dental management.
  • Preventive Dentistry Scaling, polishing, oral health assessment, and preventive dental care.
  • Prosthodontics Fixed prosthodontic procedures and restorative rehabilitation.
  • Pediatric Dentistry Dental assessment and treatment of children, with attention to age-appropriate patient management.
  • Special Care Dentistry Dental management of patients with autism spectrum conditions and individuals requiring individualized approaches to dental care.
  • Medically Complex Patients Dental care considerations for patients with diabetes mellitus and hypertension.
  • Palliative Care Dentistry Oral healthcare considerations for patients requiring palliative and supportive care.
Holding certifications in Basic Life Support (BLS) and Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR), he places particular importance on patient safety, clinical responsibility, and compassionate treatment.

Humanitarian Healthcare & National Outreach Impact

48 Free Outreach Camps
45,000+ Patient Health Checkups
BDT 4 Crore+ 40M+ Cumulative Value

Humanitarian service is one of the defining pillars of Dr. Adnan's professional identity.

He has participated in and contributed to the organization and delivery of 48 free healthcare and dental outreach camps across Bangladesh, particularly reaching rural, underserved, and resource-limited communities.

Through these initiatives, the combined outreach teams have conducted more than 45,000 patient health checkups, extending access to essential healthcare, preventive screening, dental services, health education, and community-based interventions.

His humanitarian contributions also extend to charitable healthcare mobilization and service delivery, with a documented cumulative value exceeding BDT 4 crore (BDT 40 million). Supporting certificates and documentary records are available.

These initiatives reflect his belief that access to healthcare should not be restricted by geographical barriers or financial circumstances. His commitment to community service is rooted in the conviction that healthcare professionals have responsibilities extending beyond the walls of their clinics.

Professional Leadership & Scientific Engagement

Alongside his clinical and humanitarian commitments, Dr. Adnan has undertaken leadership and organizational responsibilities within Bangladesh's medical and dental professional community.

He has served as Director of Venue and Director of Finance for the National Dental Congress 2025 and 2026, contributing to the operational planning, financial coordination, and organizational execution of major scientific and professional gatherings.

Within the Bangladesh Medical Students' Society (BMSS), SCOPE, he has held leadership responsibilities as Local Committee President and Liaison Officer, contributing to organizational development, public health initiatives, and international collaboration.

His international engagement includes representing Bangladesh at the IFMSA March Meeting in Istanbul, Türkiye, in March 2025, participating in an international platform for global health dialogue, medical leadership, and cross-cultural collaboration.

He also maintains professional affiliations and engagement with The Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh (RCSEd), IADS Bangladesh, ISSUP, and English Carnival Bangladesh.

Research, Public Health & Academic Involvement

Dr. Adnan's interests extend beyond individual clinical practice into preventive healthcare, community-based research, scientific education, and multidisciplinary public health initiatives.

His experience includes contributions to public health surveys involving more than 142 respondents and coordinating scientific educational programs involving over 200 medical students and intern doctors at Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujib Medical University (BSMMU).

These experiences have strengthened his interest in evidence-informed healthcare, population-level health interventions, preventive dentistry, and the relationship between oral health and systemic health.

Professional Philosophy & Future Vision

Dr. Adnan believes that dentistry is more than the treatment of oral diseases; it is a profession built on trust, scientific knowledge, empathy, precision, and lifelong learning.

His approach to patient care emphasizes understanding individual needs, communicating treatment options clearly, respecting patient autonomy, and providing appropriate care based on clinical assessment.

His long-term professional vision is to advance his expertise in Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery while contributing to the development of special care dentistry, humanitarian healthcare, and global public health.

Through clinical practice, scientific engagement, international collaboration, and sustained community outreach, he aspires to contribute to a healthcare system where professional excellence and social responsibility progress together.

“For Dr. Adnan, the true measure of a healthcare professional lies not only in the treatments delivered, but also in the trust earned, the lives improved, and the communities reached.”
